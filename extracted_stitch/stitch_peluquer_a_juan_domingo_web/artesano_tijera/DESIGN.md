## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet confidence of an upscale, artisanal grooming studio rooted in craft, precision, and personal hospitality. Designed for Juan Domingo, the identity balances traditional barbershop heritage with contemporary minimal luxury. It rejects both sterile corporate modernism and hyper-masculine, aggressive vintage clichés. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Editorial: clean architecture paired with tangible warmth, generous breathing room, refined bespoke detailing, and natural organic materiality. The user experience evokes the tactile calm of entering a secluded atelier—scented with bay rum and cedar, marked by warm low-reflection lighting, polished brass fixtures, and soft leather.

Targeted at individuals who appreciate unhurried service, artisanal mastery, and dignified grooming, the interface instills trustworthiness, discretion, and quiet elegance. Interactions are measured and deliberate, avoiding flashy or skeuomorphic gimmicks in favor of micro-refinements, precise borders, and seamless transitions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intimate, nocturnal atmosphere structured around deep ebony and warm charcoal, elevated by burnished brass and deep forest green.

- **Primary (`#C5A059`):** Brushed amber brass. Employed intentionally for high-intent interactive states, focus indicators, active appointment indicators, and premium emblems.
- **Secondary (`#2D3B36`):** Deep forest green. Provides organic grounding, utilized for elevated surface containers, select badges, and background contrasts on featured editorial modules.
- **Tertiary (`#F3EFE6` / `#F9F6F0`):** Warm ivory and cream. Acts as the primary typographic luminance and high-contrast text color against dark backgrounds, preventing the stark optical vibration of pure white.
- **Neutral (`#121212` / `#1A1A1A` / `#222222`):** Obsidian and rich ebony foundations. The primary canvas rests on `#121212`, rising to `#1A1A1A` for cards and `#222222` for hover states and subtle divider lines (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`).

Functional accents:
- **Success / Confirmed:** `#3E5C4B` (Muted Laurel)
- **Warning / Rescheduled:** `#C88D48` (Cognac)
- **Error / Canceled:** `#9E4747` (Deep Terracotta)

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses heritage craft supported by modern clarity.

- **Headlines (Newsreader):** A tailored editorial serif with graceful italic swashes and literary proportions. Used across hero headers, service titles, and testimonial moments. Italics are reserved for emotional punctuation (e.g., *artesanal*, *cita previa*, *la experiencia*).
- **Body & Functional Labels (Manrope):** A geometric-grotesque hybrid that maintains legibility under low-light conditions. Manrope handles operational clarity: service duration, pricing tables, booking forms, and system microcopy.
- **Labeling Style:** Section subheaders and administrative tags use `label-sm` rendered in full uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.12em`), creating an editorial catalogue feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid on desktop (max width 1200px) that condenses to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous negative space around cards and editorial copy mirrors the calm pacing of a private appointment. Content aligns to an 8px modular baseline system:
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Fixed canvas max-width of 1200px with centered alignment, 48px outer margins, and 24px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Fluid layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Multi-column services collapse into dual-column grids.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** Single-column stack with 20px margins and 16px gutters. Form controls and date pickers adapt to edge-to-edge sheet layouts.

Spacing between major narrative sections adopts expanded rhythmic pacing (`space-xl` scaled up to `5rem` via composite stacking) to ensure services and bespoke treatments feel distinct and unhurried.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with low-contrast brass and warm charcoal boundaries, avoiding high-drop commercial shadows.

- **Base Layer (0dp):** Canvas foundation set to Obsidian `#121212`.
- **Card / Surface Layer (1dp):** Ebony `#1A1A1A` framed by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(243, 239, 230, 0.07)`). Subtle ambient downward drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (2dp):** Charcoal `#222222` with warmed highlights. Border transitions to `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Overlay & Modal Surfaces (3dp):** Deep Forest / Ebony hybrid `#161B19` with a subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`), anchored by a dark backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design uses tight, tailored micro-radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`), evoking precisely planed wood, hand-cut card stock, and refined leatherwork. 

- Interactive inputs, tags, service panels, and image frames strictly utilize `rounded-sm` (4px).
- Modal sheets and floating banners scale slightly to `rounded-lg` (8px) to soften their presence against the structured page grid.
- Full pills are reserved solely for live service status indicators and selected time-slot chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in brushed brass (`#C5A059`), text in ebony (`#121212`), font `Manrope` 600. Border radius 4px. Hover transforms smoothly to luminous brass (`#D4AF37`) with a subtle glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.5)`, text `#F3EFE6`. Hover fills with `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)` and border brightens to full opacity.
- **Ghost:** Text `#F3EFE6`, no border, underlines on hover with brass `#C5A059`.

### Chips & Time Selectors
- Compact rectangular chips with 4px radius. 
- Inactive state: `#1A1A1A` background with `1px solid rgba(243, 239, 230, 0.12)`, text `#F3EFE6`.
- Selected state: Forest green `#2D3B36` background with amber border (`#C5A059`), displaying text in warm cream.

### Cards (Services & Barber Profile)
- Surface `#1A1A1A` with a 1px border (`rgba(243, 239, 230, 0.06)`). 
- Features service title in `Newsreader`, duration and pricing in `Manrope`.
- Interactive hover lifts the element by 2px and shifts the border to brushed brass (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Dark obsidian base `#151515`, height 48px, horizontal padding `1rem`.
- Inset border `1px solid rgba(243, 239, 230, 0.14)`. Text in `#F3EFE6`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to amber brass (`#C5A059`) with no harsh outline rings, using an inner shadow `0 0 0 1px #C5A059`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 2px corners (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- Outline in warm muted brass, fill in ebony `#1A1A1A`. 
- Active state fills with `#C5A059`, containing a crisp ebony check icon.

### Bespoke Pattern: Service Price Grid
- High-contrast editorial ledger style: Service title in Newsreader Serif, connected via an understated dotted rule (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) to the price in Manrope SemiBold, followed by a primary "Reservar" micro-action button.
